This table · how Portland runs

CadenceOnce a month. Two hours.Late afternoon, after the workday winds down. The date and the hour land on this page first.
SeatsEight chairs.Small on purpose, so everyone gets airtime and a hand. If the table fills, the waitlist works, and a second table can open.
The workProjects, not lectures.Bring something real you are building, or want to. We work it together, show the stack live, and write down what worked.
The session · two hours
0:00Round the table. Who you are, what you are building, where you are stuck.
0:20The stack, live. One of us walks through a real setup end to end, warts and all. Ask anything.
1:00Build together. Pair up on the projects in the room. Try it, break it, write down what happened.
1:45The close. Everyone answers one question: what will you actually try before next month?

The first few sessions will be a little choppy while the table finds its shape. That is the point. The tools are new enough that we learn them together, peer to peer.

Between meetups

The table keeps talking in the clubhouse, the club’s own chat. Two rooms belong to Portland:

#portlandDates, run sheets, and who is bringing what. #portland-projectsThe build logs: what you tried, what broke, what worked.

Come once and you are a member. Your key to the clubhouse is handed out at the table.
