101 · The curriculum

Six things AI can do for you this week.

Real tasks from real shops. Each takes about ten minutes to try, and every card ends with a prompt you can steal.

1

Write the thing you keep putting off

Estimates, listings, the friendly‑but‑firm payment reminder, the apology that keeps a customer. You talk, it drafts, you polish.

Steal this prompt“Rewrite this message so it is friendly but firm, and shorter: [paste it]”
2

Answer every customer

Replies to reviews and inquiries, quotes turned around the same day, a FAQ built from the questions you answer every week.

Steal this prompt“Here is a one‑star review. Draft a calm reply that owns the mistake and offers a fix: [paste it]”
3

Do a month of marketing in an hour

Social posts that sound like you, flyer copy, and your Google profile finally filled out.

Steal this prompt“Write 8 Facebook posts for a [your business] in [your town]. Plain local voice, no hashtag soup.”
4

Shrink the paperwork pile

Photograph a stack of receipts and get a tidy list. Summarize a lease. First‑draft an invoice.

Steal this prompt“Turn these receipt photos into a list with date, vendor, amount, and category.”
5

Read the fine print for you

Contracts in plain English. Three insurance quotes in one table. What that new rule actually means for your shop.

Steal this prompt“Compare these two quotes in a table and tell me what questions to ask before I sign.”
6

Take back your evenings

Dinner plans a kid will actually eat, the trip itinerary, homework help, the gift idea. Work is not the only thing that gets easier.

Steal this prompt“Plan five weeknight dinners a picky ten‑year‑old will eat, with one grocery list.”
101 · The Brown Bag

One hour. Six problems. Zero lectures.

6:00The six things, live. Quick real demos of all six, from shops like yours.
6:15Pick yours. Everyone names the one eating their week.
6:20Do it, together. Pull out your real task. Someone sits with you until it is done.
6:55The close. Everyone answers one question: what would you actually try?

Classes run at Bricks Coworking & Innovation Space in the Hathaway Creative Center, downtown Waterville.

Bring a laptop, or just your phone. One hour, and one problem off your plate. That is the whole point.

After 101

From lunch to lunch counter.

201 · Build with AI

Graduate from asking to delegating. Build agentic workflows, give your agents a harness so they are safe to trust with real work, and start a GitHub so nothing you build gets lost.

301 · Run on AI

The whole back office: quoting, invoicing, marketing, bookkeeping, dispatch. Your hours go where judgment lives.

The method never changes: manage an agent like a person. Brief it, delegate to it, review its work.
